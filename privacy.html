A3 Assistant privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who uses it

A3 Assistant is a student course project. It is used only with one test Google account created for the course, which contains synthetic data. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read and compose drafts): to summarize new mail, flag urgent messages and prepare reply drafts.
  • Google Calendar (read only): to list events, find free time and send meeting reminders.

How data is used

  • Email and calendar content is processed only to produce briefings, reminders and drafts for the account owner.
  • Email is never sent without the owner's explicit approval, and only to addresses the owner allowlisted.
  • Content is not sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.
  • To generate text, content may be sent to the language-model providers the assistant uses (NVIDIA NIM, or a model running on the owner's own computer).
  • Operational logs record events (for example "email scanned, risk score 2") and never message text.

Google API Services User Data Policy

A3 Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted when the course project ends.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
